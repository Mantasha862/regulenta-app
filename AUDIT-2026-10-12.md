Read-only audit. Nothing was changed in Supabase, the app or production.

- The live Supabase project "REGULENTA AI" (ref xabjjoxpunpbhdkmwkvi): tables, columns, migrations, edge functions, branches, extensions and security advisors, all through read-only listing calls.

## 1. Verdict
**The product is not functional end to end.** The core of the live database is there:
- multi-tenant tables
- row-level security on every table
- a rule-approval function with an evidence guard
- empty tables for regulatory intelligence, reminders and notifications.

The working app is small (about 400 lines) and covers four things:
- login
- creating a workspace
- adding and editing companies
- a reviewer screen over the research queue.

Everything else exists only as the static Claude Design prototype or as specifications:
- deadlines
- reminders and email
- the notification centre
- the regulatory intelligence centre
- the corporate secretarial workspace
- evidence management
- the AI assistant
- pricing and trial.
